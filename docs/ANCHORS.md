## Provenance of this public history

The timestamps in `anchors/` were produced on the original files as they existed on the dates shown. Before this repository was made public, bylines and a few citations in the early drafts were edited; the code, the specification and every file in the current tree are unchanged. The original files whose hashes are anchored are kept by the author and can be disclosed to prove those dates.
