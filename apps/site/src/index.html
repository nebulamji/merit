reading the book…

The Book. Each mark is one certified act of work, drawn from how it was made: rewrites loop back over themselves, pauses lift the pen, pasted text runs straight as a ruler, forgiven slips are tied off in gold, and faint threads show who recognized whom. Marks arrive in order and are never erased, and every new one turns the whole.

Merit

The output no longer proves the ability. A model can produce the essay, the code, the song. What it cannot produce is your history of making them.

Merit records how work was done, on your own device: corrections, revisions, pauses, pastes, timing. Never the words. You sign the record with a key only you hold, and its fingerprint is timestamped in Bitcoin so it can never be backdated. What you get is a certificate: evidence of a process, checkable by anyone, owned by you.

Verify, then inscribe

Drop a certificate here with its anchor proof (.ots), plus either a recognition (.recognition.json) or your earlier certificates and their proofs. Everything is checked in your browser first, and nothing leaves this page unless you inscribe.

    What it takes to enter

    The Book admits work, not traffic. A certificate is inscribed only when all three hold, and the server checks each one again. No one reviews anything by hand:

    Real work. At least 250 characters, mostly typed rather than pasted.
    Anchored. Its timestamp is confirmed in a Bitcoin block, not just submitted. That takes a few hours after you sign, and it means nothing here was backdated.
    Recognized, or earned by time. Either someone already in the Book signs that they recognize it (merit recognize cert.json), which is drawn as a thread from their mark to yours, or you earn entry yourself: three of your own certificates, each real work and each anchored, spread over at least a week of Bitcoin blocks. Time is the one cost that cannot be faked here. Each person can recognize three certificates every thirty days, and up to twenty-five people a day can enter by time.

    The first recognizer is the author of the paper; their threads run from Mark 0 at the center. If you know no one in the Book yet, ask for recognition: work that already qualifies waits on a list that members see in their Merit app, and one of them can recognize it with a click.

    What this is

    A certificate proves who signed a record of a session and that every number in it follows from a committed log. It is evidence of process, not proof of authorship. Its strength is cumulative: more sessions, more ways of capturing them, more people who recognize the work.

    Slips you correct and move past count in your favor. Slips you leave are part of your signature, never a penalty. Nothing is ever deleted: a correction points to what it corrects. A record that can be reset to look clean carries no information.

    The Book is a public register of certificates whose authors chose to inscribe them. Each mark changes the state of the whole: the drawing's turn is read from the latest link of a chain that runs back to Mark 0, the paper itself. Your browser re-reads the chain from the beginning every time this page loads.

    The art pays the people in it

    Merit is never for sale. The Book is. Each day the Book as it stands is auctioned as a single page, and at every hundred marks it becomes a Portfolio whose price rises with each edition collected. Half of every sale goes to the people whose marks are in it, an equal share per mark, paid by contract to the wallet each author chose. If you have a mark, you can also hold it in your own wallet, bound to you for good. Collect.
